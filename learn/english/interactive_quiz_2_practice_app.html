Unit 4D (Track 4.12) Road Rage: Abigail & Ben (Part 1)

A (Abigail): You won't believe what just happened.

B (Ben): What's wrong?

A: I was waiting at a traffic light and I guess I was a bit distracted because I didn't move immediately when the light turned green.

B: That happens all the time!

A: But then the woman behind me gets out of her car and starts banging on my window, yelling at me.

B: No way!

A: I just drove off but I'm still shaking.

B: Why are people so aggressive when they're driving?

A: I don't know.

B: Come. Sit down.

A: I guess it is stressful. When you're driving, you're constantly under pressure even if you're not aware of it.

B: It's true. I think there's more to it than that though. Once, I read a study that said if you think of your car as an extension of your personality, you're more likely to lose your temper on the road.

A: I'm not sure I follow.

B: If you see your car as part of yourself, then anything that puts your car at risk is like a threat to you, personally.

A: Hmm... that may be true. Even even-tempered people can lose control on the road.

Unit 4D (Track 4.13) Road Rage: Safe Driving Course (Part 2)

A: You know I started that short course on safe driving this morning?

B: Tell me about it! And what was the first topic of the lesson?

A: You'll never guess who was there! Our "friend" - you know, the angry driver!

B: No way! Did she recognize you?

A: As soon as she saw me, I couldn't believe my eyes! The only empty seat was right next to her!

B: That must have been so awkward.

A: He showed us a video with statistics about angry drivers. I bet it was really uncomfortable.

B: Are you serious? Was she angry?

A: No, she apologized. She came up to me at the end of the lesson and said she'd just been fired, so she wasn't thinking straight.

Unit 6D (Track 6.9) A Suitable Roommate (Part 1 - Asking Abigail)

Ben: Erm... there's something I've been meaning to ask you.

Abigail: What?

Ben: How would you feel about having another roommate? A third roommate?

Ben: Yeah. There's this guy at work who's just moved from LA. He really needs a place to live and we do have an extra room.

Abigail: I guess we could use the money, especially now the rent is going up. What's he like?

Ben: He seems nice. He's more fun than most people in their forties.

Abigail: He's in his forties?! Wouldn't it be weird to live with somebody who's old enough to be our father? Regardless of how nice he is?

Ben: Just because he's older doesn't mean we can't get along. You've got older friends.

Abigail: Yeah, but hanging out is one thing, sharing an apartment quite another.

Ben: True, but it might be nice to have somebody older and more responsible around.

Abigail: Responsibility has nothing to do with age! What if he doesn't watch the same TV shows we do? Or like the same music?

Ben: You like Ed Sheeran and I can't stand him. We're still friends, right? People can get along no matter how different their tastes are.

Ben: For your information, he's a huge Ed Sheeran fan.

Abigail: He is? But how do you know he won't mind sharing an apartment with people half his age?

Unit 6D (Track 6.10) A Suitable Roommate (Part 2 - After the Interview)

Ben: So, what did you think?

Abigail: Well, he seems nice, but did you see the look on his face when he saw the mess in the kitchen?

Ben: Yeah, maybe we seemed a bit too immature. Well, say that he turns us down...

Abigail: ... which I think he will... what next? We'll either have to look for a new roommate or find a way to save some money.

Abigail: We could always switch to a cheaper internet provider.

Ben: But even if we save 10 or 20 dollars, what difference would it make?

Abigail: It's something! And there are other things we can do. Like, why not use LED lights instead of normal bulbs?

Ben: You mean the ones that are cool to the touch? Is it worth it though? They're so expensive to buy.

Abigail: They'll save us a ton of money in the long run. The point I'm making is that if we're serious about saving money, every little bit counts.

Ben: No, I get it. It's just we'll need to spend an extra $200 as of next month, and I'm not sure we'll be able to save that much. So even if Paul turns us down, we might have to look for a new roommate?

Ben: I think so.
